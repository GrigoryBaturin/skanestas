How to use

The calculator illustrates how an investment of 10.000 USD or more could have performed over three (3) investment horizons – 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months – based on historical statistical data on the 12-month period of 2025. To use the calculator, enter your investment amount and select your desired investment horizon. The results are simulated and provided for illustrative purposes only. This tool is intended to help you understand historical patterns and potential ranges of returns, not to predict future performance.
